02 · Career objective

Where I'm headed

Draft written from Chris's résumé and lab work. Chris should rewrite this in his own voice: the rubric asks for his thoughts about his future goals, not a résumé objective.

I want to build the systems that keep other systems steady.

The first time our balance controller caught the rotary pendulum and held it upright, it clicked for me that control engineering is where the math stops being abstract. A pole in the right half-plane is a pendulum falling over; moving that pole is what keeps it standing. I want a career built around that feeling: taking something that wants to be unstable and making it dependable.

Right now I'm most drawn to controls and automation engineering: designing and tuning feedback loops for motion systems, robotics and aerial vehicles, and programming the PLCs that run real production lines. Working on a GuardLogix cell showed me how much of industry depends on logic that has to be correct and safe every single cycle.

After graduation I'd like to join a team where I can learn from experienced controls engineers, take ownership of real hardware, and keep building depth in modeling, estimation and embedded implementation. Longer term, I want to grow into an engineer who can carry a system from first equations to commissioning, and who explains it clearly to the people who rely on it.
